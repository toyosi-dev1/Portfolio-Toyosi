/about

Who I am

Designing visuals for ideas that don't exist yet.

I'm a final-year Computer Science Education student in Lagos, building a practice at the intersection of AI-assisted creativity and front-end development. I use AI as part of a larger creative process — concept development, art direction, and visual storytelling — not as a shortcut around it.

On the technical side, I work in React and JavaScript, with AI-assisted backend logic where it's useful. My prompt engineering practice covers lighting setups, lens language, material behavior, and consistency techniques like seed locking and reference-image anchoring — the same discipline I bring to design systems and digital experiences.
